# 📌 Project Overview

Insurance organizations rely on timely and accurate reporting to monitor policy performance, understand claim trends, and identify areas of increased financial risk. This dashboard was designed to provide business stakeholders with a centralized view of key insurance metrics, enabling them to evaluate customer behavior, policy performance, and claim activity from a single reporting solution.

The report combines operational KPIs with interactive visualizations to support data-driven decision-making while allowing users to explore trends across multiple business dimensions.

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to develop an interactive executive dashboard capable of helping insurance stakeholders:

- Monitor insurance policy activity
- Track claim volume and average claim costs
- Compare policy performance across customer segments
- Analyze vehicle-related claim trends
- Identify high-risk insurance categories
- Explore customer demographics
- Evaluate premium performance
- Support operational and underwriting decisions

# 🎨 Design Approach

The dashboard was designed to support executive decision-making by organizing visuals around business questions rather than technical report names.

Interactive filtering allows stakeholders to quickly explore policy performance across different customer groups while KPI cards provide an immediate overview of overall business performance.

Visual hierarchy and consistent color usage were used to improve readability while highlighting the most important operational metrics.


# 💡 Potential Business Insights

Using this dashboard, business users can quickly identify:

- Vehicle categories associated with higher claim costs.
- Customer segments responsible for the largest share of claims.
- Differences between policy volume and claim activity.
- Premium performance across customer groups.
- Insurance usage categories with elevated claim frequency.
- Potential areas requiring underwriting review.
